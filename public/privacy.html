TagMatch Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

TagMatch helps you catch grocery overcharges by comparing shelf tags with your receipt. This policy explains what information the app handles. TagMatch is operated by Zibblio ("we").

What stays on your phone

Your shelf tags, receipt lines, tag photos, trip history and refund notes are saved only in your browser or app storage on your device. We do not have an account system and we do not receive or keep this history. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling deletes it.

Photos you choose to scan (Pro)

When you use photo reading, the photo you take is sent over an encrypted connection to our server and passed to our AI provider, Anthropic, to read the item names and prices. We do not store the photo. Anthropic processes it under its commercial terms, which do not allow it to train models on this data. Only take photos of tags and receipts; avoid capturing other personal information.

Payments (Pro)

Subscriptions are handled by Stripe. Stripe collects your payment details and email; we never see your full card number. We receive a Stripe customer ID and subscription status so the app can unlock Pro. Your Pro code is a signed version of that customer ID.

Other data

Our hosting provider (Netlify) keeps standard server logs, such as IP address and request time, for security and reliability. We do not use advertising trackers and we do not sell personal information.

Children

TagMatch is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Your choices

You can use TagMatch without photo reading or payment. To cancel a subscription, use More → Manage subscription, or contact us. To ask about or delete any information tied to your Stripe customer record, email us.
