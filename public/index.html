How TagMatch works

  1. While you shop, snap the shelf tag for any item whose price you're counting on.
  2. After checkout, photograph the receipt.
  3. Tap Check. TagMatch shows anything that rang up higher than the tag, plus what to say at customer service.

Tip: keep TagMatch one tap away. On iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app.
